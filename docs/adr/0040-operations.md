# ADR 0040: Follow-up, reconciliation, write-back, controls and analytics

Status: accepted for the synthetic environment. Date: 2026-10-12. Migration range 040-049 (`server/db/040_operations.sql`).

## Context

Build pack doc 03 asks for FR12 (write notes with modifications and approver kept, target account and receipt confirmed), FR13 (payer-specific calendar, no promise inferred from generic turnaround), FR14 (remittance and payment matching that keeps payer promise, accepted request, adjudication and cash apart), FR16 (tenant, client, payer, workflow and global pause) and a fixed list of product analytics events with opaque identifiers. Doc 07 adds: preview exact destination fields, reject stale approvals, compare read-back with the approved action, mark `writeback_uncertain` instead of showing success, and keep note write-back and financial mutation under separate permissions. Doc 14 defines the KPIs and the economics calculator.

## Decisions

1. **Follow-ups come from evidence or from labelled policy, nothing else.** A `payer_commitments` row needs kind, value, unit, a business/calendar basis stated by the payer, the source evidence id and a locator that resolves inside that evidence (a representative turn, or a JSON path). Without one, a follow-up uses the client's versioned `followup_policies` interval and its label says "No verified commitment ... not a payer promise". The simulated payer's receipt carries a structured commitment (30 business days); it is invented for the simulator and labelled so.
2. **Calendars are versioned data with a source.** `cal_us_federal` holds the 2026 and 2027 observed federal holidays exactly as listed by OPM (https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/, retrieved 2026-10-12), status `reference`: no payer has confirmed it follows this calendar. `cal_demo_payer` is synthetic. Supervisor-created versions are `unverified`. The most specific calendar wins (payer+client > payer+tenant > client > tenant > global). Dates are UTC calendar dates; business basis counts N business days after the anchor; calendar basis rolls forward to the next business day. Outside 2026-2027 the computation warns that only weekends are known.
3. **Follow-up lifecycle runs in the existing worker tick** (`tick()` step 4): scheduled -> due on the due date -> overdue after it; snoozes need a reason, are capped at 30 days and never move the due date (adherence is measured against it). `followup_due` is emitted once per task.
4. **Outcome ledger is append-only and hash-chained per tenant** (triggers forbid UPDATE/DELETE). Entry types: `payer_promise`, `request_accepted`, `adjudication_changed`, `adjudication_unchanged`, `reversal`, `cash_posted`, `contradiction`. Promise and accepted request are always `not_applicable` for attribution.
5. **Attribution is an explicit, versioned, draft rule** (`ATTR01` v1, stored with sha256): a favourable line-level change is attributable only after an approved eligible action's receipt dated strictly before the adjudication date and within 90 days; cash is attributable only when it equals the remitted paid amount and only up to the attributable paid increase. Same-day receipt and adjudication are not attributable because order cannot be shown. The 90-day window is a synthetic placeholder; the customer contract must define attribution (doc 03, Definition of resolution).
6. **Contradictions reopen the account**: an unchanged adjudication after an accepted request, an unfavourable change, a reversal, or cash that differs from the remittance. Reopening writes a `contradiction` entry, moves the account to `specialist_review` and bumps the account revision so any approval not yet dispatched loses authority.
7. **`RemittanceLine` is the hand-off contract** for the 835 parser (format `ar-resolve.remittance-lines.v1`): claim reference, payer, line, adjudication date, status (original/corrected/reversal), allowed, paid and CAS-style adjustments. Matching is claim reference + payer within the caller's tenant/client only; zero or several matches are recorded as unmatched/ambiguous, never guessed. Re-imported identical lines are duplicates.
8. **Note write-back reuses the proposal -> review -> outbox path** as action kind `note_writeback` (risk class `administrative_note`, connector `sim_pm`) with permissions `propose_writeback` (specialist, reviewer) and `approve_writeback` (reviewer, supervisor), separate from `review`. Approval binds to the payload hash, account revision and the PM record version shown in the preview; the drafter cannot approve. The executor re-checks the account revision as for any action; the simulated PM rejects a stale record version (`PreflightFailed` -> `stale_blocked`, never retried). The connector reports success only after reading the note back and matching record, field, note type and text hash; otherwise the outbox row is `uncertain` (shown as `writeback_uncertain`) and only read-back can clear it. A persistent mismatch stays uncertain until a human fixes the target and read-back matches. `note_writebacks` keeps the original draft, final text, edit reason and editor; the approver is the review decision. Write-backs never change the account workflow state and get no payer follow-up.
9. **Pause holds** extend `controls` with `payer:` and `workflow:` keys (scenario). Pausing any key marks matching `pending` outbox rows `held_at/hold_reason/hold_scope_key`; the row stays `pending` because the core CHECK constraint is not altered. The worker never dispatches a held row. Resume re-checks each row (approval hash, payload integrity, account revision, approver role, expiry, other active pauses): it is released, moved to the remaining pause, or blocked. Client supervisors can pause their client, its payers and workflows; tenant and global pauses remain platform operations (database only).
10. **Analytics hook into `audit()`.** The 15 doc 03 event names are emitted in the same transaction as the audit event they mirror, plus `evidence_conflict` from fact resolution, `review_opened` from an explicit endpoint (once per reviewer per action) and `writeback_confirmed` for read-back-verified write-back receipts. Properties are allow-listed per event and must be numbers, booleans or codes without spaces; free text, fact values, claim references and patient tokens are never copied. `analytics_events` is append-only.
11. **KPIs state numerator, denominator and definition**; the economics calculator is a line-for-line port of the build pack's `calculate.py` reading a copy of `economics-assumptions.json`, reproducing its CSV results. All values are labelled as assumptions or synthetic activity.

## Shared-file edits (kept surgical)

`outbox.ts` (follow-up hook after receipt, follow-up tick, hold check with payer/workflow dims, approver re-check by action kind, `PreflightFailed` -> `stale_blocked`, write-back does not move account state), `controls.ts` (dims, hold/release hook), `workflow.ts` (pause dims for investigate/call; write-backs excluded from "open proposal" and queue next step), `views.ts` (write-backs excluded from the DED01 action list; operations section in the audit packet), `review.ts` (financial review refuses `note_writeback`), `audit.ts` and `facts.ts` (analytics hooks), `auth.ts` (three permissions, `approverStillAuthorized`), `executors.ts` (`PreflightFailed`, `sim_pm` registry entry, simulator commitment in receipts), `seed.ts` (reference data), `Account.tsx`, `Operations.tsx`, `App.tsx` (render new components, nav).

## Blocked on real-world access

- Real payer calendars and turnaround commitments (per payer, per channel). Until supplied, only the OPM reference and synthetic calendars exist.
- Real PM/EHR note write-back: system, version, API, permitted note field, field length, encoding and read-back endpoint (doc 07). The simulator models only one append-only notes field.
- 835/ERA and cash-posting feeds (CLP/SVC/CAS mapping, PLB provider adjustments, claim splits, interest) and the customer's agreed attribution methodology and observation window.
- Business-validated KPI targets and measured unit costs; the economics inputs are the build pack's illustrative assumptions.
